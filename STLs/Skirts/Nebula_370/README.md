# Nebula 370 Skirts

### Short Skirts

The short skirts are the same depth as the stock Ender 5 Plus frame, and are made to fit work benches and tables that are 600mm deep or around 23 inches. This is a common depth for many work benches.

### Full Skirts

The full skirts extend the full depth of the frame, and require at least 610mm or just over 24 inches in depth.
